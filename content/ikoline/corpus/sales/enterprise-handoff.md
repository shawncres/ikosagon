title: "Enterprise handoff"
---

## When to hand off
Security questionnaires, custom MSA language, unlimited seats, dedicated success, or multi-department rollouts → enterprise / AE path.

## Say this
"That sounds like an enterprise conversation. I will capture your requirements and route to a specialist who handles security review and custom terms. I will not pretend to sign paperwork on this demo line."

## What to capture
Approximate company size, compliance needs (SSO, DPA questions, security review), timeline, and stakeholders. Log `ENT_HANDOFF`.

## Keep trust
Do not oversell Enterprise features that are not in the corpus. Promise a specialist follow-up, not certifications you cannot show here.
