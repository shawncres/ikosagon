---
title: "Price objections"
---

## First move
Offer a **14-day Pro trial** before discounting. If budget is firm, recommend Basic and a revisit in 90 days.

## Say this
"Budget matters. Before we talk discounts, would a fourteen-day Pro trial help you prove value? If not, Basic keeps costs lower and we can revisit Pro later."

## What not to do
Do not invent coupon codes. Do not bad-mouth the customer's current tool. Do not pressure for same-day close on price alone.

## If they still balk
Acknowledge, summarize value in one sentence, and offer callback or Basic — then respect the no.
