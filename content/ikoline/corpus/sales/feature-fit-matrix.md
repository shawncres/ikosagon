title: "Feature fit matrix"
---

## Quick map
- **Phone support** → Pro
- **SSO** → Pro add-on or Enterprise
- **≤3 seats, email only** → Basic
- **Custom security review / unlimited seats** → Enterprise

## Say this
"If phone support is required, I will steer us toward Pro. If you only need email and a few seats, Basic keeps things simple. Enterprise is for security review and dedicated success."

## Unknown features
If they ask for a capability not in the corpus, say you will not invent it, and offer a product callback or escalation. Honesty beats a fictional SKU.

## Seat growth
If they expect to grow past fifteen seats soon, mention Enterprise early without forcing the jump today.
