## Warranty
Hardware carries a **12-month** limited warranty covering manufacturing defects. Accidental damage is not covered unless Support Plus is active.

## Shipping SLAs
Standard ground: 3–5 business days. Expedited: 1–2 business days. Weather and carrier delays are outside guarantee windows.

## Login & billing
Password resets send a 15-minute link. Duplicate charges: open a billing case; do not issue goodwill credits without supervisor approval over $50.
