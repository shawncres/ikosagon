---
title: "Returns & refunds"
---

## Return window
Unused items in original packaging may be returned within **30 days** of delivery. Opened software and personalized goods are final sale.

## Refund timing
Refunds post to the original payment method within **5–10 business days** after the warehouse scans the return.

## Exceptions
Defective items qualify for replacement or refund beyond 30 days when a warranty applies. Escalation is required for chargeback threats.
