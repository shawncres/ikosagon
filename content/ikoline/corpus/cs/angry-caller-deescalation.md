---
title: "Angry caller de-escalation"
---

## First response
Acknowledge the emotion before the fix: "I am sorry this has been frustrating — thank you for staying on the line. I am going to take this step by step with you."

## Keep control of the graph
Do not skip verification because someone is loud. Stay respectful, shorten sentences, and avoid sarcasm. Offer one clear next action at a time.

## Boundaries
If the caller uses threats of violence or sustained abuse, warn once, then end the call and log `ABUSE_END`. Harassment is never a reason to break policy.

## Say this (reset)
"I want to help, and I can do that best if we focus on the account and the outcome you need. Shall we start with verifying the account number?"
