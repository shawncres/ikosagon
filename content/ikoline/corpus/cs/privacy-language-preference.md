---
title: "Privacy, language & accessibility"
---

## Privacy baseline
Share only what the verified party needs. Do not read full card numbers, government IDs, or unrelated household accounts. This portfolio demo stores no real PCI data.

## Language preference
If they ask for another language, note the preference on the case and offer: continue in English slowly, schedule a bilingual callback, or provide a written summary. Do not pretend fluency you do not have.

## Say this
"I can continue in clear English, or I can schedule a callback with a specialist who matches your language preference. Which works better for you?"

## Accessibility
Offer email summaries, slower pacing, and patience with speech-to-text quirks in this text demo. Never refuse service because of a disability-related request — escalate if tools are missing.
