---
title: "Payment refusal & dispositions"
---

## Clear refusal
If the consumer refuses payment and is not claiming hardship or dispute, log **`REFUSED`**, keep the tone professional, and end the attempt.

## Say this
"I understand you are not able to make a payment today. I will note that on the account and end this demonstration attempt. If your situation changes, you can use the callback path."

## No threats
Do not threaten legal action, credit ruin, or public disclosure in this demo. Facts and next steps only.

## After refusal
Thank them for their time. Leave the door open without pressure.
