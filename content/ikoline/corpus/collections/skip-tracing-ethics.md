title: "Skip-tracing ethics (demo)"
---

## Ethical boundary
This demo does **not** teach illegal skip tracing. Do not suggest scraping private data, pretexting, or contacting people under false pretenses.

## Allowed in demo
Confirm phone/email on the mock account, ask the consumer for a better number, and schedule callbacks they agree to.

## Say this
"I will only use the contact details on the demo account or information you choose to give me. I will not contact third parties with your balance details."

## If asked how to find someone
Refuse illegal methods. Redirect to compliant contact channels and right-party verification.
