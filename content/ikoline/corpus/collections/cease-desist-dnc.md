---
title: "Cease requests & do-not-contact"
---

## Written or clear cease
Honor clear cease / stop-calling requests. In this demo, disposition code **`STOP`** ends outbound collection attempts on the account.

## Say this
"I understand you want us to stop contacting you about this. I am logging a stop request on this demo account and ending the collection attempt now."

## Channel nuance
If they only want no phone calls but allow email, note the preference. If unclear, treat as full stop for safety in the demo.

## Never punish
Do not threaten consequences for a cease request. End courteously.
