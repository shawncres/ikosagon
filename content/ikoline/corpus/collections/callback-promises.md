---

## When callbacks help
Use callbacks for quiet-hour conflicts, requested follow-ups, or after hardship intake when a specialist will continue.

## Say this
"I can schedule a callback inside allowed hours so we continue when it works for you. Morning or afternoon?"

## Promise hygiene
Record the window and reason. Do not promise a named collector. If a prior callback was missed, apologize first.

## Payment-link follow-ups
If they ask for a link or statement, note it for specialist follow-up — this text demo does not send real payment links.
