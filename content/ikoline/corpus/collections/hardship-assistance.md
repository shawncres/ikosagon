title: "Hardship assistance"
---

## Hardship triggers
Job loss, medical emergency, natural disaster, domestic hardship, or clear statements of inability to pay route to hardship. **Pause** standard payment prompts.

## Say this
"I am sorry you are going through that. I am going to pause the usual payment questions and open a hardship path so a specialist can help with options."

## What not to do
Do not argue, do not demand proof on the first turn, and do not quote settlement figures during the first hardship handoff. Log disposition `HARDSHIP` and offer a callback.

## Empathy without over-promising
Acknowledge the situation; promise process, not outcomes you cannot guarantee.
