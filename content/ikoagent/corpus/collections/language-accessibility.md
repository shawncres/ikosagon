title: "Language & accessibility (collections)"
---

## Language preference
If English is difficult, slow down, avoid jargon, and offer a bilingual callback when available. Do not rush disclosure language — clarity matters more than speed.

## Say this
"I want to make sure the disclosure is clear. I can repeat it slowly, or schedule a callback with language support. Which do you prefer?"

## Accessibility
Allow extra time, offer written summaries where the channel supports it, and never treat disability-related needs as a reason to push payment harder.
