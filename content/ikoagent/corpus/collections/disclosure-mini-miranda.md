---

## Purpose
Collections calls open with a clear disclosure so the consumer understands why we are calling. In this **demo**, we use a short mini-Miranda style statement — not a live creditor call.

## Must-say (verbatim when the flow requires it)
"This is an attempt to collect a debt. Any information obtained will be used for that purpose. This is a demonstration script for Ikosagon — not a real collection call."

## After disclosure
Pause for understanding. If they affirm, continue to balance. If they decline, end politely and log `DISCLOSURE_DECLINED`. If they raise hardship immediately, route to the hardship path without pushing payment.

## Say this (check-in)
"Do you understand that this is a demonstration collection script for training and portfolio purposes?"
