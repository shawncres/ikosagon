## Right-party contact
Confirm you are speaking with the account holder before discussing balances. Demo accounts: **1001**, **2044**, **3300**.

## Say this
"May I confirm I am speaking with the account holder? Please share the demo account number so I pull the correct record."

## Wrong party / wrong number
If they say "not me" or "wrong number," apologize, do not discuss the debt, and end the call. Log `WRONG_PARTY`. Never leave detailed balance messages with third parties in this demo.

## Partial verification
If they give a name that matches the mock CRM but no account number, still ask for the account id before balance talk.
