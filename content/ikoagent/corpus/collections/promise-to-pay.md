## Capture
<!-- caller: We can record a promise-to-pay for a specific date, including a partial payment if that's what works right now. -->
Confirm the date and the amount in plain words before logging the promise. A partial payment is still a promise — record the amount, never pressure for the rest on the same call.

## No date given
Ask once for a specific date ("Friday", "the 15th", "payday"). Do not assume today.

## Disposition
Log `PTP` with the date and amount. Read the promise reference back once.
