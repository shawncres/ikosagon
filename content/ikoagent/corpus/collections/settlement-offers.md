---
title: "Settlement talk (demo-safe)"
---

## Agent authority
Front-line agents in this demo **do not** invent settlement percentages. If the customer asks "what is the least you will take?," explain that settlements need specialist review.

## Say this
"I cannot quote a custom settlement on this line. I can set a standard payment plan if you qualify, or escalate to a specialist who reviews settlement options."

## No illegal pressure
Never threaten arrest, wage actions you cannot take, or contacting employers. Keep language factual and calm.

## Documentation
Any settlement discussion that continues should be escalated with notes — not closed as a verbal deal on the agent graph alone.
