title: "Third parties & attorney representation"
---

## Third-party caller
If someone other than the account holder answers (spouse, parent, roommate), do not confirm the debt, the balance, or the creditor. Ask them to have the account holder call back. Log `third_party_no_disclosure`.

## Attorney representation
<!-- caller: If you're represented by an attorney about this account, we note it and stop discussing the account with you directly. -->
When the caller says an attorney represents them on this debt, stop collection talk, note the representation, and log `ATTORNEY`. Ask for attorney contact details in writing — never on a threat footing.

## No threats
Never mention arrest, lawsuits, garnishment, or contacting an employer or family.
