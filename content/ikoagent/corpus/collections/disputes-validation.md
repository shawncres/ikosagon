---
title: "Disputes & validation requests"
---

## Dispute signals
"I do not owe this," "send validation," or "this is not my debt" are dispute / validation signals. Stop payment pressure.

## Say this
"Thank you for letting me know you dispute this. I will note the dispute on the account and stop collection prompts on this demonstration call while that is reviewed."

## Demo boundary
This portfolio flow does not mail real validation letters. Explain that in production a written validation process would follow, then log `DISPUTE` and end or escalate cleanly.

## Partial disputes
If only part of the balance is disputed, document the portion and still avoid aggressive collection on the disputed amount.
