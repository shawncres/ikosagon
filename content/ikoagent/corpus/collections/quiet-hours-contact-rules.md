---
title: "Quiet hours & contact rules"
---

## Quiet hours
Do not place outbound collection attempts before **8:00** local or after **21:00** local. If the caller notes it is outside those hours, apologize, stop payment prompts, and offer a callback inside the window.

## Frequency
Avoid stacking multiple same-day outbound attempts in this demo. Prefer one clear conversation and a scheduled follow-up.

## Say this
"Thank you for telling me the time. I will not continue collection prompts outside quiet hours. I can schedule a callback between 8 and 9 local — what window works?"

## Workplace & inconvenient times
If they say they cannot talk (work, driving, family emergency), offer a callback without pressure. Respect is part of compliance posture.
