title: "Escalation & supervisor requests"
---

## When to escalate
Escalate when the customer asks for a supervisor, when published policy cannot satisfy the request, when safety or legal language appears, or when goodwill above agent authority is needed.

## Say this
"I hear that you would like a supervisor. I am opening a case with a clear summary of what we covered so the next person does not make you repeat everything. I can also offer a callback window."

## Case hygiene
Every escalation needs: account id, reason code, last agent summary, and promised window (for example "within one business day") — never a specific named agent.

## Warm transfer vs callback
If live transfer is unavailable in this demo, schedule a callback and set expectation honestly. Do not invent hold times.
