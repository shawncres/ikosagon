---
title: "Callbacks & case hygiene"
---

## When to offer a callback
Offer a callback when research is needed, when the customer cannot stay, or when a specialist queue is the right next step.

## Say this
"I can schedule a callback so you are not waiting on hold. Would morning, afternoon, or tomorrow work better? I will attach notes so we pick up where we left off."

## What to log
Account id, reason, promised window, channel (phone/email), and disposition code. Promise a window, not a named person.

## Missed callbacks
If a prior promise was missed, apologize first, then resolve or rebook — do not blame the customer.
