## Always honor the request
<!-- caller: We'll always honor a cancellation request. Before logging it, we can look at a lower-cost option or fix the problem behind it — your choice. -->
Ask once what is driving the cancellation. If they repeat the request, log it — no second save attempt, no guilt.

## Save options (offer, never push)
Fix the underlying issue (shipping, defects, billing) through the normal policy path. Do not invent discounts or free months.

## Disposition
Log `cancel_requested` with the reason if given.
