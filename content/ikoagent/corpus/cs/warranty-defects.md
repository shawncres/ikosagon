---
title: "Warranty & product defects"
---

## Coverage
Hardware carries a **12-month** limited warranty for manufacturing defects. Accidental damage, liquid exposure, and unauthorized modifications are not covered unless Support Plus is active on the account.

## Defect intake
Ask: when did the issue start, any error codes, and whether troubleshooting steps were tried. Offer RMA replacement when the defect is confirmed in-window.

## Say this
"Based on what you described, this sounds like a covered manufacturing defect. I can start a warranty replacement. You will receive an RMA number and shipping instructions by email."

## Out of warranty
Explain options honestly: paid repair quote, refurbished exchange if stocked, or escalate for goodwill only when a supervisor approves. Never invent coverage.
