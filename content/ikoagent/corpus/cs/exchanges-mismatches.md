## Wrong item received
<!-- caller: If we sent the wrong item, we ship the right one at no charge. -->
If the packing slip does not match what arrived, apologize, open a replacement shipment at no charge, and arrange pickup or discard instructions for the incorrect item.

## Say this
"I am sorry we sent the wrong item. I will arrange a replacement for what you ordered and give you clear next steps for the package you received."

## Size / color exchanges
<!-- caller: Within 30 days, one exchange at the same price is free. -->
Within 30 days, exchanges that stay at the same price tier are free once. Price differences require a refund of the original and a new order, or a supervisor for a one-time adjustment under $50.

## Out of stock
Offer: waitlist with ETA, comparable in-stock alternative, or full refund. Do not promise a restock date you cannot verify in the corpus.
