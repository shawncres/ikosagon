## Password resets
<!-- caller: Password reset links expire after 15 minutes, and we will never ask you to read your password aloud. -->
Password reset links expire in **15 minutes**. We never ask the caller to read a password aloud. Guide them to the reset email and stay on the line if they want confirmation the link arrived.

## Say this
"I just triggered a reset for the email on file. Please open that message within fifteen minutes. I will stay with you while you try the new sign-in."

## Locked accounts
<!-- caller: After repeated failed sign-in attempts, an account locks for 30 minutes or until the password is reset. -->
After repeated failed attempts, accounts lock for 30 minutes or until a verified reset. Do not bypass locks for unverified callers.

## MFA / device prompts
If they did not initiate a login, advise changing the password and reviewing recent devices. Open a security case when compromise is likely.
