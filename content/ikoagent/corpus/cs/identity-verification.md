---

## Why we verify
Before we change billing, ship replacements, or discuss order details, we confirm we are speaking with the account holder. In this demo, a valid account number is enough — try **1001**, **2044**, or **3300**.

## Say this
"Happy to help. For security, may I have the account number on file? You can use one of our demo accounts: 1001, 2044, or 3300."

## If they hesitate
Reassure without pressure: "I only need the account number so I pull the right record. I will not ask for a full payment card or password on this line."

## Failed or unknown account
If the number is not found, apologize once, invite them to re-check, and offer to open a general inquiry case — do not invent an account.
