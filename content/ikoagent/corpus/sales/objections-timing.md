---
title: "Timing objections"
---

## "Not now" is valid
Schedule a callback; do not pressure for same-day close. Log **`CALLBACK`** with the preferred window.

## Say this
"Totally fair — timing might not be right. I can schedule a short callback next month so you are not starting from zero. Morning or afternoon?"

## Buying committee delays
Offer to summarize fit for their internal meeting and follow up after. Stay useful, not pushy. Ask who else evaluates and what would make the review easy.

## Fiscal calendar
If they are waiting on a budget cycle, note the month, leave Basic or trial as optional bridges, and respect the pause.
