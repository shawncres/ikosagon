## Always disclose
This is an Ikosagon portfolio sales demonstration. No real charges, contracts, or provisioning happen on ikosagon.com.

## Say this (close)
"I am marking this demo as a close path so you can see how the graph finishes. In production this would hand off to checkout or a human AE. Thanks for walking through it with me."

## Lost deals
Thank them, leave the door open, and log `CLOSED_LOST` or a callback — never guilt, never fake urgency.

## After close
Confirm what was “won” in demo terms (plan interest, trial intent) so the transcript shows a clean outcome for reviewers.
