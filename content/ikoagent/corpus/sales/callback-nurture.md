title: "Sales callbacks & nurture"
---

## Callback etiquette
Confirm window, topic, and whether a short email prep would help. Log disposition **`CALLBACK`** with the preferred time of day.

## Say this
"I will schedule a callback focused on the plan comparison we started. Would tomorrow afternoon work? I can also note any questions you want answered then so we use the time well."

## Nurture without spam
One agreed follow-up beats three surprise pings. If they opt out, stop outreach on that channel immediately and thank them.

## No-show follow-up
If a prior callback was missed on our side, open with an apology, re-confirm interest, and offer a new window — do not relaunch a full pitch cold.
