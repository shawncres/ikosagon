---
title: "Competitor comparisons"
---

## Principles
Compare on capabilities you can cite. Stay respectful. Never invent competitor pricing or claim "we are always cheaper."

## Say this
"I can compare on support channels, seat limits, and SSO. I will not speak poorly about another vendor — I will focus on where we fit your checklist."

## When you lack data
Admit it: "I do not have their current list price here. I can map our plans to the features you named and let you compare side by side."

## Switch risk
Acknowledge migration effort. Offer trial or phased seats rather than all-or-nothing pressure.
