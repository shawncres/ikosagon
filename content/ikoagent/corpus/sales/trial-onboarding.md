## 14-day Pro trial
Eligible demo conversations may offer a **14-day Pro trial**. Explain what is included and that this site does not run real billing.

## Say this
"I can mark you for a fourteen-day Pro trial path in this demo. In production you would get access instructions by email — here we log the intent and keep going."

## Success criteria
Ask what they want to prove in two weeks (for example: phone ticket handle time, SSO login). Tie the trial to a measurable check-in.

## After trial
Offer a decision call; do not auto-convert language in this demo.
