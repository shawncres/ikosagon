---
title: "Upsell without pressure"
---

## When upsell is appropriate
Only after needs are clear and Basic is undersized (seats, phone support, SSO). Frame as optional next step.

## Say this
"Based on needing phone support and more than three seats, Pro is the better fit. If you prefer to start smaller, Basic is still available — your call."

## No dark patterns
No false scarcity, no fake countdown timers, no hiding the cancel path. This is a portfolio demo of ethical sales control.

## Downgrade grace
If they choose Basic after hearing Pro, affirm the choice and leave an easy upgrade path.
