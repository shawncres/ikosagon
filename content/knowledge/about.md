# About Ikosagon

Ikosagon is Shawn Cooper's Toronto studio for software that upgrades processes people already run. The public site is ikosagon.com.

## Who this is for

Small and medium businesses, homeschool families, and operators who need a working tool more than a slide deck. Typical first jobs: booking and intake, FAQ answers from real policies, quote drafts, inbox triage, and thin AI layers on existing workflows.

## Who builds it

Shawn Cooper (Toronto / GTA). Background in QA automation, insurance systems, and product engineering. Stack in daily use: Python, Next.js, TypeScript, React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, and model APIs (xAI, Groq, Gemini).

## How work starts

Start at /contact. Share the business type, the hours you are losing, and whether you need a week-long prototype or a longer build. Discovery is paid when the scope is unclear. No invented case studies or prices live on this site.

## What this site is not

It is not a hosted large language model. Chat on this site answers only from Ikosagon markdown notes and project briefs. It will say when something is not in the notes and point to the contact form.
