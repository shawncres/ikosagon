# Services

Ikosagon designs and ships software that upgrades existing processes for production use — lean, cost-aware, and built to survive real volume on-prem or in the cloud.

## Offers

- Product engineering and MVP builds
- Frontend systems and UI that stay readable on a phone
- RAG and grounded chat over your policies and docs, using open-source or cloud models and vector databases (e.g. Chroma)
- Agentic chatbots with reliable tool calling — including on small or low-parameter models — and continuous improvement loops so bots harden reactively over time
- Custom CRM integrations with strong QA and test discipline
- AI-integrated remote-desktop PWAs and internal operator tools
- Natural-language contact-center–style flows (LangChain, ElevenLabs, Suno where voice or media fits)
- Edge and embedded work: NVIDIA Orin-series devices; ARM and ESP32 microcontroller firmware for IoT
- Automation around quotes, FAQs, booking, and messy documents
- xAI ecosystem adoption guidance for SMB through enterprise-shaped scopes

## Engagement shape

Short paid discovery when the problem is fuzzy. Fixed-scope prototypes when the workflow is obvious. Contract or freelance when you need someone to own the first production version. Selected full-time roles when the fit is clear.

## Constraints we respect

- Keys stay on the server. Visitors never receive provider secrets.
- Answers about Ikosagon must come from site notes, not guessed clients, employers, metrics, or prices.
- Canadian SMBs should assume data handling is discussed up front (what is stored, where it lives, how to turn it off).
- On-prem vs cloud is a design choice, not a slogan — lean cost and high-volume process fit come first.
- Vercel Hobby is the current public host for this portfolio. It can proxy a model API. It cannot run a local 3B+ model.
