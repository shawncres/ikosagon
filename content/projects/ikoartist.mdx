## Problem
People should be able to lean into a song Ikosagon makes for them without creating an account, filling a CRM form, or leaving an email.

## What this product is
A low-friction web product for **almost anonymous song participation**, backed by an **agentic music pipeline**:

1. **Listen** — a ranked playlist in the player. An agentic system produces songs and **cycles them in**; **better songs get higher priority**
2. **Click a taste path** — genre → mood → era → geo-ish → related artists → albums
3. **Get 3 track leans** — algorithmic suggestions with a short “why” from your clicks
4. **Finish anonymously** — no email needed. Your taste path still feeds the agentic pipeline so a song can get made. Leave email only if you want opportunity follow-ups

Optional voice / photo are add-ons. Skip anytime. More answers → better match. This page does not auto-spend on Suno / ElevenLabs / Gemini when you finish — generation and playlist ranking run in the product pipeline, not as a live click-to-generate meter here.

## Principles
- Product first: participate in a song, not a heavy talent-CRM funnel
- No account required; anonymous path works end-to-end
- Agentic pipeline → songs cycle into the player; stronger tracks rise in the playlist
- Click web every step; free-text walls avoided
- Fresh-enough free catalog (iTunes Search seed + optional live refresh; MusicBrainz best-effort) with an **as of** date — no Chartmetric / Spotify premium, no invented chart ranks
- Anonymous finish is enough for the song path; email is optional (opportunities only)
