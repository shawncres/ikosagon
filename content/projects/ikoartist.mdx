## Problem
People should discover music they’d actually want to hear — and help shape a song Ikosagon makes for them — without creating an account, filling a CRM form, or leaving an email.

## What this product is
A low-friction web product for **almost anonymous song participation**, powered by the **agentic Ikosagon engine**:

1. **Listen** — a **leaderboard-style playlist** ranked by views. Better tracks rise as people listen.
2. **Build your song brief** — click-first, in three layers, each step skippable with an optional “add your own”:
   - **Sound**: a short question tree to find your **three favorite artists**. You pick an anchor, answer a few questions about what that pick can’t explain (raw vs polished, words vs groove, mainstream vs underground, instrumentation, energy and tempo), then add a second artist close to the first and a third further out, and say what one has that another doesn’t. From that we compute a **center** (where your taste sits) and a **range** (how far your three picks spread).
   - **Themes**: tap the lyrical themes you gravitate toward (heartbreak, hustle, nostalgia, faith, party, escapism, social commentary and more). We compare them with theme tags on your artists’ best-known tracks and add a clearly dated chart context when a free public chart is reachable.
   - **Audience** (optional): coarse bands only — age band, region, language, listening contexts — with a plain note on why we ask. No account, nothing exact, and it stays out of the transcript.
3. **Get 3 track leans** — one from the overlap of your three artists, one from each edge of your range, each with a written “why” built from the numbers
4. **Finish anonymously** — no email needed. Your brief still feeds the agentic engine so a song can get made. It takes some time: curated choices plus adapting agentic processes produce the hit. Leave email only if you want opportunity follow-ups

Optional voice / photo are add-ons. Skip anytime. More answers → clearer signal. This page does not auto-spend on Suno / ElevenLabs / Gemini when you finish — generation and leaderboard ranking run in the product pipeline, not as a live click-to-generate meter here.

## Principles
- Product first: discover taste and participate in a song, not a heavy talent-CRM funnel
- Low commitment — answer as many questions as you want
- No account required; anonymous path works end-to-end
- Agentic Ikosagon engine produces the hit (takes some time); curated choices + adapting processes
- Leaderboard-style playlist by views — better tracks rise
- Click web every step; free-text walls avoided (each step has an optional, length-capped “add your own”, sanitized, never required)
- Honest data: the sound map is **our editorial estimates**, not measured audio features; the three “best tracks” per artist are our assumption, not stream data. Chart context comes from a free public iTunes feed, shows an **as of** date, and never shows ranks — no Chartmetric / Spotify premium, no invented chart ranks
- Audience answers are bands only, anonymous, and kept out of the transcript
- Anonymous finish is enough for the song path; email is optional (opportunities only)
