IkoAgent answers from your procedures, not from memory.

A small business already has the answers in return policies, price sheets, hours, and product notes. We at Ikosagon built IkoAgent so staff and customers can ask in plain language — and see the document the answer came from.

## The problem

The usual failure is not a missing chatbot. It is two employees giving two different answers about a refund, a recall, or the hours on Tuesday. The policy exists. It is just buried in a file nobody opens during a rush.

## What the client gets

- One desk for the questions customers already ask.
- An answer that cites the procedure it used.
- A refusal when the documents do not cover the question, instead of a confident guess.
- A backlog of the questions the desk could not answer. That list is the next procedure to write.
- Updates by editing a document. The shop does not retrain a model to change a return window.

## How a shop uses it

1. Gather the documents the staff already trust: policies, SKUs, hours, warranties, recalls.
2. Ask the questions customers actually call about.
3. Read the cited passage next to the answer. If the passage is wrong, fix the document.
4. Each week, read the questions that were refused. Write the missing procedure, add the file, and those questions start getting answers.

## The sample

The public example is a fictional outdoor shop, **Northwind Outfitters**. Its procedures cover returns, a membership club, three store schedules, tents, garment repair, and a stove recall. It is a demonstration, not a client engagement.

![IkoAgent sample desk for the fictional shop Northwind Outfitters, with customer themes around the message bar and the shop procedures listed on the left.](/projects/ikoagent/desktop-desk.svg)

A visitor can ask things like:

- Which fuel canister lot codes were recalled?
- How long is a Boot Lab appointment, and which shop offers it?
- What is the Summit Club annual fee, and when is the card billed?
- How many days does a customer have to return an unused item?

![IkoAgent answering which fuel canister lots were recalled for the Northwind sample, with the cited passages in the trace column.](/projects/ikoagent/desktop-answer.svg)

![Close view of a customer question about recalled fuel lots, the cited answer, and the passages used.](/projects/ikoagent/answer-and-trace.svg)

An unrelated question, such as the population of a city, is refused. The desk does not spend an answer on something the procedures do not contain.

![The same procedure desk on a phone, with the topic buttons around the message bar.](/projects/ikoagent/phone-desk.svg)

## Proof

**39 of 39** checks on the included question set. For every policy question, the correct procedure file was among the three passages retrieved. Off-topic questions were stopped before a model wrote an answer.

This is black-box QA on the sample desk — measured retrieval and refusal — not a customer case study and not a rounded-up marketing metric.
