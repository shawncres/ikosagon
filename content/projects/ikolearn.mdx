IkoLearn is a chat-style learning companion with a visible, evolving learning profile. [Open IkoLearn](/ikolearn/).

## A profile you can see

Most learning tools keep their picture of you hidden. IkoLearn draws it. Your first name sits in the middle of a point cloud. Around it are subjects, each with its own colour, and around those are the topics you have explored, in shades of that colour. Faint dotted branches are related topics you have not tried yet.

The cloud is not decoration. It is the structure the companion reads before every reply, so it knows what you have done, what came easily and what felt hard.

## It grows as you talk

After each reply, the companion also notes which topics the conversation touched and how it went: explored, a strength, something hard, or just curiosity. IkoLearn adds or strengthens those topics under the right subject, opens a new subject when one is really needed, and turns a dotted branch into a real one when you explore it. New points pulse for a moment, so you can watch the map fill in.

## What you can do

- **Talk.** A normal chat: ask anything, get a short, clear answer that streams in as it is written.
- **Tap the cloud.** Tap your name or any point for a short note on what the companion notices, or two or three ideas for what to try next.
- **Make a card.** A short learning card on a topic, with one key idea and one small thing to try. Cards attach to their point on the cloud, so you can come back to them.
- **See what is next.** Ask for related topics and they appear as dotted branches, mixing easy neighbours with skills that will matter later.

## Built for the future of learning

We want learning that starts from solid foundations and widens toward the skills that will matter: building, thinking clearly, working with AI. A cloud that grows with the learner makes that path visible, for the learner and for a parent reading along.

## How it works

- Replies come from Google's Gemini, through our own small server routes. Our key stays on our server.
- Your name, conversation, cloud and cards are kept in your browser only. We do not store them.
- Replies can be wrong. A parent or teacher is the best check.

## Status

An early, unlisted test. One learner, one device, no accounts. Next: views for parents, richer cards and deeper branching.
