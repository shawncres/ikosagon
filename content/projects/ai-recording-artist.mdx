## Problem
Artists and collaborators need a simple place to hear AI-assisted recording demos and start a conversation — without a heavy production portal on day one.

## What this page is
A public home for the AI Recording Artist experiment: a working demo player now, and a collaboration inquiry flow next. Tracks below are short placeholders so the player can be exercised end-to-end; they will be replaced with real AI artist demos.

## Principles
- Listen first: clear playlist UI, no account required
- Honest demos: placeholder clips are labeled until real material ships
- Collaboration later: a guided inquiry flow will collect the basics before emailing Shawn
- No invented pricing: scope and rates stay out of public copy until agreed in writing

## Status
In progress. Audio player and project card are live in this PR. Collaboration QA + email automation are intentionally stubbed.
