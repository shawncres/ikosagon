## Problem
Artists and collaborators need a simple place to hear AI-assisted recording demos and start a conversation — without a heavy production portal or public rate card on day one.

## What this page is
A public home for the AI Recording Artist experiment: a working playlist as proof of results, and a guided talent intake chatbot that emails Shawn a summary for review. Active artist representation and any costed agentic work come after approval.

## Principles
- Listen first: clear playlist UI, no account required
- Real tracks: playlist entries map to committed MP3s under `public/projects/ai-recording-artist/`
- Intake without pressure: genres, favorites, age category, location/geo, tastes — all skippable; the more you share, the better the fit
- Optional media: short browser voice sample and photo when feasible on Vercel Hobby email limits
- Human review first: no live Suno / ElevenLabs / Gemini generation or auto star-potential scoring on this page yet
- No invented pricing: scope and rates stay out of public copy until agreed in writing

## Status
In progress. Playlist and talent intake chatbot are live on this page. Proprietary taste/geo algorithms, career suggestions, and active representation workflows are future — after Shawn’s explicit yes on costed work.
