## Problem
Probability and prediction markets (and sports odds) drown operators in competing forecasts. Raw consensus blends noise with signal; fixed weights go stale as markets and seasons move. Teams need a way to rank sources, aggregate probabilities, and improve that ranking over time — without pretending a single model already “solved” the market.

## What this product is
Hexcast is Ikosagon’s R&D line for **ranked probability aggregation**: take many probability estimates (models, books, markets, experts), score how they have ranked against outcomes, then combine them into a clearer aggregate view. The stack sits on Ikosagon’s **proprietary ranking system** and is aimed at prediction/probability markets and sports.

In plain language: Hexcast ranks who has been useful lately, weighs forecasts accordingly, and keeps re-learning that ranking as new results land — a self-improving aggregation loop rather than a static average.

## Principles
- Rank first, then aggregate: source quality is measured relative to outcomes, not assumed equal
- Proprietary ranking stays proprietary: public copy describes the idea, not the secret sauce
- Markets + sports focus: same aggregation ideas, different event calendars and data shapes
- Honest R&D: no invented win rates, ROI, backtests, clients, or prices on this site
- Explainable enough to discuss: operators should understand *what* is being ranked and aggregated, even when the ranking method itself is not open-sourced
