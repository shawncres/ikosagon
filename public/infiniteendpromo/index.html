01 · FlyPlayable

Boost is the whole point.

A fast, weighty ship that banks, rolls, loops and flies inverted. Thread asteroid fields at speed while the music builds, and burn spice to slow time a breath before the crash. Then dive to a planet and skim its surface.

02 · ExplorePlayable

A universe that remembers you.

Every system grows from a seed: same coordinates, same world, forever. Glacier fields, ember forges, creature nurseries, ship graveyards. Pilots hail you with stories and choices. Break a rock today and it's still gone tomorrow.

03 · SurvivePlayable

Mine it. Feed it. Keep flying.

Laser ore out of the rocks, then fly through drone swarms that turn it into fuel, weapons energy or hull. Trade at the hubs, take courier jobs, survive the odd skirmish. The meters are generous, so new pilots can just fly.

04 · ListenPlayable

A soundtrack no one else will hear.

Music generated live as you fly. Every system has its own tempo, key and chords: calm when you drift, building when you boost. And the Ikosagon radio sounds like a real one. Flip it off and on to find a new track.

Roadmap

The route, honestly.

No dates we can't keep. Each stage ships before the next one starts.

  1. Stage 1 · The prototypeLive

    An endless seeded universe, flight, spice, mining, swarms, trading, encounters and missions, a generated soundtrack and the radio. Desktop and mobile browsers, with regular updates.

  2. Stage 2 · Into the headsetNext

    On-headset testing on Meta Quest and performance tuning on real graphics hardware.

  3. Stage 3 · Life aboardIn development

    The customizable cab, card duels on the dash against pilots you meet, and the handheld console.

  4. Stage 4 · Early accessPlanned

    First store release and optional pilot accounts that carry progress between devices.

  5. Stage 5 · The NetworkPlanned

    Leave your deck on the network and it fights for you: passive duels against other pilots' decks while you fly. Real stations on the radio.

  6. Beyond · The long haulVision

    Deeper systems, a PC app, and a universe that keeps growing with its pilots.
