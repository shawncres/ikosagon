Privacy

Short version: IkoLearn keeps your learning on your device. Nothing you add here (your name, age range, chats, cloud or cards) is stored on Ikosagon's servers.

On your device. Your first name, your recent conversation, your learning cloud and your cards, plus the topics you said you enjoy, are saved in this browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. "Start over" clears them. Clearing your browser data does too.

When you send a message. Your message, your first name, your last few messages and a short summary of your cloud (subject and topic names) go to our server, which passes them to Google's Gemini API to write the reply. Our server does not keep them. On the free tier Google may use what is sent to improve its products, so please do not type anything private: no surnames, addresses, school names or contact details.

Your age range, if you choose. You can tell IkoLearn your age range (under 10, 10–13, 14–17, 18+ or prefer not to say), or skip it. It is saved only on this device, and you can change or clear it any time with Iko or the “Your age” link. When you open a lesson, ask for help with one or take a test, the range goes along inside that request so the lesson is written at the right level (shorter sentences, simpler words, more or fewer examples). Our server passes it to the AI for that one reply and does not keep it. It never limits which topics you can learn.

No location. IkoLearn never asks where you live: no location permission prompt and no location lookup. To suggest a few starting ideas while your cloud is small, it looks at your browser's own time zone and language setting on this device. That stays on the device: it is never sent to our server, never saved and never shown. If an older version of IkoLearn saved an age band or a rough region on this device, it is ignored now and cleared the next time your progress is saved.

What you enjoy. If you tell IkoLearn what you're curious about, those words are sent with the request that builds your first topics, so your path starts from things you like. Your path only starts from what you offer: if you skip the question, nothing is sent until you write something. If you also answer the optional question about something you'd like to grow in yourself, it goes in that same request. The topic names it makes are kept on this device.

Examples. The easy-click examples under the first question come from our server: one shared set, written by the AI about once a day and the same for everyone. Fetching them sends nothing about you, and tapping one is just like typing it.

Limits. To keep the service free we count requests per IP address for a short time, in memory only, to stop overuse.

Accuracy. Replies and cards are written by AI. They can be wrong, so check anything important against a trusted source.

Who it's for. IkoLearn is for anyone curious, at any age. Parents can also use it to plan and guide homeschool learning.

Light or dark. IkoLearn follows your device's light or dark setting. If you pick one with the small sun or moon, that choice is kept in this browser only; it is not part of your cloud or of an IkoCloud file.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking scripts.
