Privacy

Short version: IkoLearn keeps your learning on your device. We at Ikosagon do not store your name, chats, cloud or cards on our servers.

On your device. Your first name, your recent conversation, your learning cloud and your cards, plus your age band, rough region and interests if you gave them, are saved in this browser's local storage so you can pick up where you left off. "Start over" clears them. Clearing your browser data does too.

When you send a message. Your message, your first name, your last few messages and a short summary of your cloud (subject and topic names) go to our server, which passes them to Google's Gemini API to write the reply. Our server does not keep them. On the free tier Google may use what is sent to improve its products, so please do not type anything private: no surnames, addresses, school names or contact details.

Age and region. IkoLearn can ask roughly how old you are, as an age band like "7 to 9" (optional). It also guesses a rough region, like "Canada / Ontario-ish", from your browser's language and time zone. There is no location permission prompt and no location lookup service, and nothing more precise is ever read. Only the age band and the rough region go to our server with your requests, so topics fit your level and what is usually learned where you live. You can change or clear both under "About you", and they are kept on this device with the rest.

What you enjoy. If you tell IkoLearn what you're curious about, those few words are saved on this device and sent with the request that builds your first topics, so your cloud starts with things you like.

Limits. To keep the service free we count requests per IP address for a short time, in memory only, to stop overuse.

Accuracy. Replies and cards are written by AI. They can be wrong. A parent or teacher is the best check.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking scripts.
