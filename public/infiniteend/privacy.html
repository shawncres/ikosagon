Privacy

The Infinite End, by Ikosagon. Last updated November 24, 2026.

Short version: your game stays on your device. The Infinite End has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and we don't collect your name, email, location or any other personal information.

What's saved on your device

Your progress (your ship, cargo, credits, discoveries and the changes you've made to the universe) and your settings are saved in your device's browser storage so you can pick up where you left off. They are never sent to us. Clearing the app's data, or resetting your progress in the game's settings, deletes them.

What the game loads from the internet

The game runs from ikosagon.com, so it needs an internet connection. When it loads, your device asks our site, and a few other services, for the files it needs:

  • ikosagon.com serves the game and the radio's track list.
  • Google Fonts serves the game's lettering.
  • Suno's servers (cdn1.suno.ai, cdn2.suno.ai) serve the music tracks the in-game radio plays.

Like any website, these services see the standard information your device sends with a request, such as your IP address and device type, and may keep it in ordinary server logs. We don't use it to identify you, and we don't sell or share anything about you.

Links that leave the game

If you open a link from inside the game, such as the music playlist, it opens that website, which has its own privacy policy.

Meta Quest

If you got The Infinite End from the Meta Horizon Store, Meta handles your purchase and your Meta account under Meta's own privacy policy. The game itself doesn't receive your Meta account details.

Children

The game doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this changes, for example if we add optional accounts, we'll update this page first. Questions: shawn@ikosagon.com
