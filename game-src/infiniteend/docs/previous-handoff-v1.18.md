## v1.18 changes (search the file for these names)
1. **Star streaks removed** (Shawn: "looks stupid"). The `fxStar` setting and the `uStk` uniform are gone. Twinkling stays.
2. **Big stars** (`bigStar()` in the sky shader; `BSK`, `bigStarsSet()` in bz_game): one or two per place (none or one beside a close star), seeded per place, sent as `uBS[8]` (direction + kind) and `uBC[8]` (colour + angular size). Kinds: 0 sun-like with six diffraction spikes, 1 red giant (mottled breathing disc, limb darkening), 2 blue supergiant (long spikes), 3 white dwarf in a ring nebula, 4 pulsar (flicker and sweeping beams), 5 protostar with bipolar jets, 6 Wolf-Rayet in a clumpy bubble, 7 binary pair. About half of places (never one with a close star) get one showpiece (kind 1, 2, 3, 6 or 7); the rest get plain suns or a pulsar. Diffraction spikes are thin, short and faint (`spk()`: width about 0.00045 rad, length capped at 0.045 rad, ×0.35), after Shawn found long screen-crossing spikes stupid; he also found too many phenomena in one sky.
3. **Close stars** (`closeStar()` in the sky shader, uniforms `uCS`/`uCC`): in about 32% of places (never planet-side) the system's own sun is drawn up close at the sun direction, so its light matches the scene. Angular radius depends on the stage (`[.1–.18]` red dwarf … `[.14–.3]` blue giant, `[.02–.04]` white dwarf); about 35% of red/orange/yellow suns appear as a giant (radius .26–.38, coarse cells, slow pulsation). The surface has convection cells (coarse for giants), sunspot groups with umbrae, bright active regions with magnetic loops, limb darkening and a bright rim; off the limb there's a corona with streamers and flame-like prominences in a few sectors. A binary system shows a small blue companion. Colours by stage in `CSC` (saturated). While a close star is up, the light shafts drop to 15% and the lens flare to 20%, so it reads as a surface rather than a searchlight.
4. **Display defaults:** HUD colour Amber, brightness 70%, size 85%, cinematic bars on. Migration `fxMig` 4 applies these once to existing players.
5. **New score** (`SC`, `scTick`, `scTrack`, `scStep` at the end of bz_game; the old sequencer, arp and drone pad are retired). This follows Shawn's Tron/Justice brief, in raw Web Audio:
   - **Seeds:** per star system, tempo 110–140 (104–113 planet-side), key, mode (minor or Dorian) and progression (`PROGS2`: i–VI–III–VII, i–v–VI–VII, two-chord vamps…). Per place, style (Tron: straight, pulsing 16th arps; Justice: broken kicks, swing, distorted chopped stabs, stronger wobble), kick/clap/ghost/hat/open-hat patterns, a bassline weighted walk, a 2-bar Markov lead motif, arp and stab shapes, wobble rate and depth, filter cutoff, distortion, delay (dotted-8th or 8th) and reverb amount.
   - **Arrangement** (`SECS`, `scNextSec`): intro → groove/build → drop → breakdown… in 8/16-bar phrases, driven by `energy` (speed and combat). Drifting holds intro/breakdown; cruising grooves and builds; boosting drops. Speed changes cut in at the next 4-bar line (boosting jumps to a 4-bar build; slowing from a drop falls to the breakdown). Builds open the filters, intensify the hats, roll the snare and end in a riser; drops start with an impact; fills come at section ends.
   - **Sounds:** kick (sine with a pitch sweep plus a click transient); sidechain pump (everything but the kick ducks under it); clap/snare (filtered noise plus a tonal body), ghost notes; closed and open hats with swing; bass (pure sub sine under detuned distorted saws through a resonant filter with envelope and tempo-synced LFO wobble); pads (four detuned saws, slow attack, long release, heavy reverb); arp, stabs and lead (unison saws/squares, snapping filters, sent to the delay); a continuous filtered-noise atmosphere bed.
   - **Chain:** voices → saturation → pump → ping-pong delay (filtered feedback) + convolution reverb (generated 3.5 s tail) → compressor/limiter (−10 dB) → master. Metered in headless Chrome: drop peak ~0.39 and RMS ~0.10 at the limiter, build ~0.24/0.06, intro ~0.11/0.014; no clipping.
   - The radio still silences it while a song is on the air; muting and the solo/cue logic still apply.
   - Test hooks: `ES.mus()`, `ES.musMeter()`, `ES.musSec(name)`, `ES.bigLook(i, kind)`, `ES.closeStar(stage, giant, radius)`.

## Late v1.18 fixes (after Shawn's review)
- **Warp gate tunnel restored (a bug from v1.15).** A code comment placed mid-line in the composite uniform setup commented out `uJ` (the jump stretch/tunnel) and `uFl` (the lens flare). Comment moved; a scan of all sources found no other code hidden behind comments. Jump sequence screenshots now match the live file.
- **Close star reworked** (Shawn: "looks horrible"): fine granulation (scale 16, 26 for hot stars, 7 for giants), small spots in groups instead of grey blotches, saturated stage colours (`CSC`), brighter emissive disc (×1.8), stronger tight corona and prominences in the star's own colour. The binary companion is now a bright point, not a flat disc.
- **Music replaced again** (Shawn: the deep sounds were obnoxious; likes The Weeknd; sent a softer Tone.js sketch). Same arrangement engine (`SC`, `SECS`, energy-driven), new sound set following his sketch: 112–128 BPM (98–108 planet-side); deep soft kick through a 120 Hz low-pass with a gentle pump (0.45 → 1 over 0.22 s); a restrained band-passed snare mostly in the reverb (on 2 and 4 in the "night" style, on 3 in "drift"); sparse dark offbeat hats (high-pass 6 kHz); clean sub bass through a 180 Hz low-pass on quarter notes around the root and fifth (with a faint octave triangle for small speakers); sine/triangle pads with chorus-like detune, 2.5 s attack, 4 s release, 7th chords; a soft triangle lead with a slowly drifting filter, sent to the delay; a soft triangle arp ("night") for The Weeknd shimmer. Reverb 4.5 s with 50 ms pre-delay; 8th-note ping-pong delay, feedback 0.28; limiter −6 dB. No distortion, wobble, stabs, impacts or saw risers (only a soft noise swell into the full section). Metered: full section peak ~0.26, RMS ~0.07.
- **Fewer massive objects bunched together:** huge rocks (`hugeAt`) only exist in every other cell on each axis (min ~1.5 km apart), with a higher acceptance rate.
- **Boost rush slower:** local boost thrust `bst · 400 · eff · (1 + .55 · bRamp)` (top local speed ~620, was ~1,800); crossing the system still runs on the long-range drive (`sysS`), unchanged. Rocks now emerge over a longer distance (size fade `.5R → .28R`, was `.36R`).
- Test hooks added: `ES.jumpTest(loc)`.

## Second round of late fixes
- **Pulsar (`bigStar`, type 4):** the beams spun a full turn every ~12 s and strobed 14×/s. Now they drift (`TIME*.012`), are shorter (`s*9`), and the flicker is a soft pulse.
- **Music clipping:** the kick's pump jumped the whole bus to .45 in one sample (a click on every beat), and the score limiter's 5 ms attack ground on the sub. The pump now eases (`setTargetAtTime` .5 then back to 1), the limiter is −4 dB, 4:1, 30 ms attack, and the kick, bass and score output are trimmed slightly. Bass voices stop later, so they fade fully before they end.
- **Boost challenge — overdrive:** hold boost past 7 s and `bOd` climbs 0→1 over 18 s. Local speed rises from ~620 toward ~960, the rock assist's sideways nudge and last-moment brake fade out, near-miss spice doubles, and fuel burn rises a little. "OVERDRIVE · ASSIST OFF" pops when it starts. Off planet-side and in light drift. Rock fade-in distance is unchanged, so rocks still arrive with warning.
- Debug hook `ES.bh(seconds)` sets the boost-hold timer.
- **Cockpit:** flight stick moved to the left and throttle lever to the right, at Shawn's request (geometry in `b_world.js` under "stick on the left"; pivots for moving parts in the cockpit vertex shader, `aU.y` 1 and 2).

  - **U** opens it full screen (`pipOpen()`, a DOM overlay at 1280×720) with tabs STATUS · HOLD · LOG · NAV · COMMS. Use ←/→, A/D, Q/E, Tab or 1–5 to switch tabs (or click one); U or Esc closes it. The world slows while it is open, as with the hold.
  - LOG collects every `tip()` and `pop()` (`pipLog`). U is in the remappable actions and the key list.
  - The 16:9 frame is meant to host more later, up to a whole game.
  - Debug: `ES.pip(tab)`.
- **Living quarters** (seen with , . / or in a headset): a bunk on the left (mattress, blanket, pillow, curtain, a shelf with books, a plant and a photo, string lights, a reading lamp, two photos on the wall), a galley on the right (counter, mug, coffee maker, notebook, open cubbies with bins and folded clothes, an under-shelf lamp), a jacket by the hatch and a rug, all lit by warm light pools. ## Fourth round
- **Title menu:** the always-on "Pilot's card" was pushing the menu off the bottom of shorter windows, hiding Settings. It's now a **View controls** menu item (07) that opens the key list as a dialog (`keysShow()`). The list is built from the live bindings: remapped keys show their new key in the second colour, and space-sim mode shows the space-sim meanings. Esc, the × or a click outside closes it. It's hidden on touch screens.
- **Aux display key is now Tab** (remappable; U still works as a quiet alias). Tab, U or Esc closes it; **1–5 pick the tab**, and the arrows, A/D and Q/E step through them. Tab no longer cycles page focus in flight.
- **Smoother frames:** the aux screen's canvas is now CPU-side (`willReadFrequently`), so uploading it no longer forces a GPU readback, and it redraws 5×/s instead of 8. Dynamic resolution was already in place (it trades resolution to hold ~57 fps); full screen on launch means more pixels, so expect it to work harder on High detail.

## Fifth round: frame rate and the cursor
- **Cockpit split into two draws.** All the quarters' materials, their warm lights and the aux screen had gone into the one cockpit shader, so every dash pixel paid for them. Now the dash draws with the lean shader (`P_COCK`; any material above 12 is plain panelling there), then the aux screen and quarters draw with `P_CAB` (the same source compiled with `#define CABIN`, which holds the quarters' materials, warm pools and CRT). The quarters' geometry sits in its own index range (`mCock.cb` up to the glass). The CRT's glass reflection no longer samples the nebula.
- **Cursor / mouse capture:** launching requested full screen and pointer lock together, and the browser could refuse the lock mid-transition. After 0.7 s the game then fell back to cursor steering, which shows the crosshair cursor and steers less directly. Now it asks for the lock again once full screen has settled, waits 1.6 s before falling back, and a click in flight always retries the lock.

## VR fixes from headset testing (2026-11-19)
- **Store app title screen:** inside the packaged app (`PWA`), the main button is **Enter VR** (`vr(1)`, falling back to desktop flight only if the headset can't start). The separate VR button, View controls and the full-screen note are hidden there. The website is unchanged.
- **Cockpit scale in VR:** the cabin was drawn at 1 unit = 1 m in the headset, which put the floor 3.7 m below the eyes and the seat 2 m below. Now `CKM = 3` cockpit units per metre: the floor is about 1.2 m below, the dash about 0.45 m below and 0.7 m ahead, and the cabin about 2.3 m wide. The cockpit and HUD eye (`cex/cey/cez`) also follows head position from the session origin (clamped to ±1.6 units), so leaning works.
- **VR menu scrolling:** both controllers were read every frame, and the idle one reset the stick latch, so a held stick stepped every other frame. Each hand now has its own latch (`xNav[h]`): one step per push, then a repeat every 220 ms after 480 ms.
- **Warp tunnel in VR:** the composite pass centred the tunnel on each eye's image centre, which differs between eyes because the projections are asymmetric (and moves with the head). It's now centred on the ship's forward point projected for each eye (`uJc`), so both eyes agree. Desktop looks the same.
- **Levelling planet-side:** on entering an atmosphere the ship rights itself over about 2 s (`rightT`): it rolls level, un-inverts, keeps its heading, and pitch is clamped to ±72°. In VR it keeps easing back to level while planet-side (no loops below the clouds in VR).
- Game-only changes: deploy `index.html`. The store package doesn't need rebuilding.

## VR round 2 (2026-11-19, from headset testing)
- **Cabin scale and the real floor:** the XR reference space is now `local-floor` (falling back to `local`). The cockpit eye's height is the room's: `CK_FLOOR (-3.64) + head height × CKM`, with `CKM = 2.5` units per metre (2.1 m floor to ceiling, seat ~0.65 m, bunk and counter ~0.9 m). ## Living universe round (2026-11-19)
- **Names from Earth (`NM` in `a_core.js`):**
  - Systems are a city with a prefix or suffix ("New Halifax", "Kyoto Reach", "Port Hudson").
  - Planets are explorers, scientists and old gods ("Shackleton", "Hypatia", "Anansi").
  - Places use a kind-specific list: rivers for belts, volcanoes and foundry towns for forges, deserts for voids, ports for hubs, lost ships for wreck fields, gardens and reefs for nurseries ("Danube Field", "Etna Works", "Rotterdam Exchange", "The Atacama Quiet", "Roanoke Graveyard", "Borneo Bloom").
  - Pilots get real-world names (`personName`), civilian ships Earth names (`NM.ship`), raiders their own (`NM.raider`), and navy frigates are "ISV …".
  - Name seeds are stirred with `nameRng` (neighbouring systems' ids are correlated). Traffic control is "<system> Control". The old colour-word prefixes are gone (`L.hue` is still computed).
- **Warp gates:** the lane graph is sparser (`linked`: an east-west spine plus 14% branches, about 2–3 lanes a system). Lanes to other systems leave only from a system's first place (its gate). Planet skies aren't on the gate list; you fly down to them. Objectives route through the system gate, and say "fly down to <planet>" for atmosphere destinations.
- **Oxygen (`ST.o2`, `o2Tick`):**
  - Drain: about 45 minutes a tank, ×1.6 with the hull under 50%.
  - Refill: below a planet's clouds at 6.5%/s ("O2 SCOOPS OPEN · REFILLING").
  - Warnings at 30/15/5% target the planet most nearly ahead.
  - At 0: 45 s of hypoxia (composite `uFy.w`: desaturate, tunnel vision, pulse), then a blackout and a tow to the system gate (−10% credits, air 45%).
  - HUD: left column is HULL, SHLD, O2, SPICE; the aux display shows O2. Debug `ES.o2(h)`.
- **The living (end of `bz_game.js`):**
  - **Persistent cast** `ST.cast`, up to 60 pilots: name, ship, faction, temperament (`TRAIT`: warm, gruff, nervous, wry, formal, ambitious), home sector, relationship −100…100, memories (`MEM`).
  - **Comms fly-ins** (`cmStart`/`cmTick`), every 2½–5 min in space: a known pilot (likelier near home) or a new one flies up and holds station ahead-right (formation off the player proxy `PL`), greets you in their voice (`VOICE`), and makes an offer from `mkOffer`: an escort, a contract, a fuel request or a tip; someone you've wronged brings a grudge instead (and maybe raiders). F (A in VR) accepts; ignoring declines after 20 s (−3 relationship). Nothing is modal. Radio lines wrap to 3 lines and stay up longer.
  - **Escorts** (`escStart`/`escTick`/`escEnd`): the caller plus 1–2 haulers make for a point 3.5+ km away (the gate, or a far point) and wait if you fall behind. An ambush (2–3 raiders with `escHunt`) comes at 30–60% of the way, with maybe a second wave at 80%. The raiders shoot convoy ships and turn on you if engaged. Pay scales with ships saved. Failure: all lost, or you 5+ km away for 25 s, or you jump. Each outcome is remembered.
  - **Memory:** shooting a cast member's ship remembers it (−30). Known pilots now and then pass you in traffic and greet you. Traffic drops in more often and talks more.
  - Debug: `ES.life('call'|'near'|'talk'|'esc'|'accept'|'amb')`.
